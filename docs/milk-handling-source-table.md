# Milk Handling on Shift · Instructor source table

**Instructor only. Not for students.** Status: in staging for clinical review and approval.

**Standard taught.** AAP is the clinical foundation. CDC is the named standard for storage timelines and quiz answers. Where AAP and CDC differ, the course teaches the CDC wording everywhere: narration, on screen text, decision sheet, and quiz. Each difference is listed below with a review flag.

**Scope stated in the course.** Routine care of healthy babies at home in the United States. For a premature, hospitalized, or medically vulnerable baby, follow the care team's plan. For formula, follow the product directions. Cases are fictional teaching examples, and "ICS workflow" items are Ivory Cradle teaching practice, not CDC or AAP guidance.

## Where the course states a source difference to students

- V2 and W2: AAP materials list some different storage times; this course teaches CDC; a pediatrician's instructions for the family come first.
- V6 and W2: leftover breast milk. HealthyChildren allows quick refrigeration for the next feeding; CDC says discard after 2 hours; the course teaches CDC.

## Items for clinical review

1. Leftover breast milk (#24): the course teaches CDC's discard after 2 hours over HealthyChildren's refrigerate for the next feeding.
2. Overlapping limits (#25): applying the earlier of the warming and leftover deadlines.
3. Shaking (#20): neutral wording; no official source found.
4. Power outage (#18): CDC's "within the next day" wording.
5. Premature and medically vulnerable infants (#31, #32): scope wording, and that clinician and product instructions take priority.
6. Fictional formula label (#29) and the ICS verification and labeling workflow (#3, #4).
7. Illustrations: bags, containers, the swirl, and formula forms are drawings rather than prop footage. Close-up prop footage can replace them after review.
