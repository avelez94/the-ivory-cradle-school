Milk Handling Decision Sheet

Storage times follow CDC. Clinical guidance from AAP.
Routine care of healthy babies at home in the U.S.

1 Before you offer any bottle

Which baby is it for?Check the name on the label when milk is stored for more than one child.
What does it contain?Expressed breast milk or prepared formula. Each follows its own clock.
What is its handling history?Expressed or prepared, frozen, thawed, warmed, offered at a feed.

Combining, transport, power outage
  • Cool fresh milk before adding it to chilled milk. The older milk’s date sets the limit.
  • Insulated cooler with frozen ice packs: up to 24 hours, then use, refrigerate, or freeze.
  • After an outage: ice crystals still present, may refreeze. Fully thawed but cold, refrigerate and use within the next day or discard.
Formula
  • Ready to feed: no water. Concentrate and powder: water exactly as the label says, water first, then powder.
  • Under 2 months, born before 37 weeks, or weakened immune system: boil water, wait about 5 minutes, mix (about 158°F), cool, test on wrist.
  • Clinician directed plan or special formula: follow it and the product directions.
