## The lesson

1. The bottle in the fridge: the fridge clip, a freeze frame asking "What would you verify before offering this bottle?", 20 seconds to answer, then the three checks (which baby, what it contains, handling history), the standard (CDC times, AAP guidance) and the scope.
2. Label and store fresh milk: hand washing, containers, label practice, the room temperature, refrigerator and freezer chart, back not door, first in first out.
3. Freeze, thaw, and transport: portions and headroom, thawing methods, the worked thaw timeline (moved Monday 8:00 p.m., thawed Tuesday 6:00 a.m., use by Wednesday 6:00 a.m.), cooler transport, and the power outage example.
4. Swirl, combine, and warm: the swirl, the shaking question, combining (older milk sets the limit), serving cold, safe warming, no microwave.
5. Infant formula: three forms, clean preparation, safe water, the teaching label exercise, extra precautions for higher risk infants, opened powder.
6. Two milk clocks: separate formula and breast milk timelines, the formula case (prepared 10:00 p.m., fed 10:40 to 11:05 p.m.), the overlap case (warmed 11:00 p.m., feed ends 11:30 p.m., 1:00 a.m. applies), and the leftover milk source difference.
7. The 2:00 a.m. bottle decision: containers A (use), B (discard), C (set aside and verify), 90 seconds to decide with chimes, a say it out loud practice, and the close.

Quiz answer key (staff only): 1 C, 2 B, 3 D, 4 A, 5 B. Explanations and citations are in `db/seed/courses.mjs`.
